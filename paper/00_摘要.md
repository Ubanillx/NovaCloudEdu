# 摘要

## 中文摘要

**论文题目：基于大语言模型的智慧教育云平台设计与实现**

教育数字化转型已成为全球教育发展的核心趋势。党的二十大报告作出"推进教育数字化"的重要战略部署，教育部于2024年启动人工智能赋能教育行动，明确提出开发教育领域人工智能大模型、推动智慧教育平台智能升级的战略目标。孙立会等学者指出，生成式人工智能融入智慧教育平台能够系统化重构教育体系，推动组织架构、教学范式、教学过程与评价方式的全方位创新与变革[26]。然而，传统教育模式在个性化需求满足、教学资源均衡分配和教学效果精准评估等方面仍存在诸多不足[27]。与此同时，以GPT-5、DeepSeek等为代表的大语言模型（LLM）凭借其强大的自然语言理解与生成能力、上下文学习能力和泛化能力，在个性化学习路径支持、教学资源智能推送和智能评估反馈等方面展现出显著优势[27]，为教育智能化提供了新的技术路径。但大语言模型自身也面临幻觉问题和知识检索精度不足等挑战，现有教育平台普遍存在AI功能碎片化、缺乏统一工作流编排能力等问题，难以满足教育场景对智能化的深层需求。

针对上述问题，本文设计并实现了"智云星课"智慧教育云平台，将大语言模型深度融入教学、学习、考试、批改等教育全流程。平台采用领域驱动设计方法，划分28个业务子域，覆盖AI对话、知识库管理、工作流编排、智能批改、在线考试、课程学习、音视频互动等核心教育场景。

本文的主要研究工作和创新点包括：（1）设计了可视化AI工作流引擎，支持16种节点类型的图形化编排，使教师能够通过拖拽方式构建复杂AI教学任务，降低了AI应用的使用门槛；（2）构建了基于向量检索与语义精排的RAG知识库系统[2]，教师可上传教学资料形成专属知识库，显著提升了AI回答的领域准确性和专业性；（3）实现了"OCR识别-AI逐题批改-知识画像构建-错题自适应推荐"的全链路智能批改方案，能够自动识别学生薄弱知识点并生成个性化学习建议；（4）开发了支持联网热点搜索、几何图形自动渲染和AI配图的智能出题系统，辅助教师高效命题；（5）实现了三级降级策略的实时音视频通话与在线直播系统，支持课堂互动和远程教学。

系统采用微服务架构进行容器化部署，同时提供Web管理端和移动端双端应用。测试结果表明，平台各模块功能完善，AI交互响应流畅，智能批改准确率较高，系统性能满足实际教学场景需求，验证了大语言模型技术在教育领域深度应用的可行性与有效性。

**关键词：** 大语言模型；智慧教育；检索增强生成；AI工作流引擎；智能批改；个性化学习

## Abstract (English)

**Title: Design and Implementation of an Intelligent Education Cloud Platform Based on Large Language Models**

The digital transformation of education has become a global trend. China's 20th National Congress report outlined the strategic initiative to "advance educational digitalization," and the Ministry of Education launched the AI-empowering Education initiative in 2024, explicitly calling for the development of education-specific AI models and the intelligent upgrading of smart education platforms. Sun et al. noted that integrating generative AI into smart education platforms can systematically reshape the educational system, driving comprehensive innovation across organizational structures, pedagogical paradigms, instructional processes, and assessment methods [26]. However, traditional educational models still face significant shortcomings in meeting personalized learning needs, equitable resource distribution, and precise assessment of learning outcomes [27]. Meanwhile, Large Language Models (LLMs) such as GPT-4 and Qwen have demonstrated remarkable capabilities in natural language understanding and generation, contextual learning, and generalization, showing significant advantages in personalized learning path support, intelligent resource recommendation, and automated assessment [27]. Nevertheless, LLMs themselves face challenges such as hallucination and insufficient knowledge retrieval accuracy, and existing educational platforms typically suffer from fragmented AI functionalities and lack of unified workflow orchestration, falling short of the deep intelligence demands of educational scenarios.

To address these challenges, this thesis designs and implements "NovaCloudEdu," an intelligent education cloud platform that deeply integrates LLMs into the full spectrum of educational processes, including teaching, learning, examination, and grading. The platform employs Domain-Driven Design with 28 business subdomains, covering AI-assisted dialogue, knowledge base management, workflow orchestration, intelligent grading, online examinations, course learning, and real-time audio/video interaction.

The main contributions of this work are as follows: (1) A visual AI workflow engine supporting 16 node types is designed, enabling teachers to construct complex AI-powered teaching tasks through a drag-and-drop interface, thereby lowering the barrier to AI adoption in education. (2) A Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) knowledge base system based on vector retrieval and semantic re-ranking is built, allowing teachers to create domain-specific knowledge repositories that significantly improve the accuracy and professionalism of AI responses. (3) A full-pipeline intelligent grading solution integrating OCR recognition, AI-driven per-question grading, student knowledge profiling, and adaptive wrong-answer recommendation is implemented, capable of automatically identifying students' weak points and generating personalized learning suggestions. (4) An AI-powered question generation system supporting real-time web search, automatic geometric diagram rendering, and AI-generated illustrations is developed to assist teachers in efficient test design. (5) A real-time audio/video communication and live streaming system with a three-tier fallback strategy is implemented to support classroom interaction and remote teaching.

The platform is deployed using a microservices-based containerized architecture and provides both a web management console and a cross-platform mobile application. Experimental results demonstrate that all modules function properly, AI interactions are responsive, intelligent grading achieves satisfactory accuracy, and the overall system performance meets the requirements of real-world educational scenarios, validating the feasibility and effectiveness of deeply applying LLM technologies in the education domain.

**Keywords:** Large Language Model; Intelligent Education; Retrieval-Augmented Generation; AI Workflow Engine; Intelligent Grading; Personalized Learning
